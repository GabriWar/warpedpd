Warped Pixel Dungeon is free to download and the dungeon is free to play in full: every floor, item, hero and ending, with no ads and nothing behind a purchase. Supporter subscription

The game offers an optional monthly supporter subscription. It is a way to fund development, not a way to buy power: no gameplay advantage, level, item, hero or area is sold, and the entire dungeon remains available to all players for free. What it covers is the relay that connects players who are not on the same network, which carries a monthly bill. Local network multiplayer is not part of the subscription and never will be.

  • What it is: Warped Pixel Dungeon Supporter, offered at several monthly pledge levels so you can choose how much to contribute.
  • Length: one month per period, renewing automatically.
  • Price: shown in your local currency on the supporter page inside the game before you confirm anything, as reported by the store you bought it from (Google Play or the App Store).
  • What supporters get: a room code that connects them to distant players, plus a voice in what gets built — a vote on upcoming features, priority handling of bug reports, input on balance and design decisions, and early access to new builds as they are prepared.

Billing, renewal and cancellation

  • Payment is charged to your Google Play or Apple ID account when you confirm the purchase.
  • The subscription renews automatically each month at the price of the tier you selected, until you cancel. On the App Store, auto-renew must be turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid the next charge.
  • Google Play: manage or cancel at any time in the Play Store app under Payments & subscriptions, or from the "Manage subscription" button on the supporter page. App Store: manage or cancel in your device's Account Settings. Cancelling stops future charges; the current period runs to its end.
  • Supporter status is verified against your store receipt and ends when the subscription lapses.
  • Refunds are handled by the store that processed the payment, under its own policy. The developer cannot issue store refunds directly.

Fair use

The game includes optional co-op multiplayer: over your local network, free for everyone, or through the developer's relay with a room code, which comes with the supporter subscription. When you play with others, do not use it to harass other players or to interfere with their devices. On a local network the session runs directly between the players' devices. Online, it is carried by a relay the developer runs, which forwards bytes between the two players without reading them; the developer therefore cannot see, moderate or intervene in what happens inside a session. The relay is provided as-is and may be rate limited, interrupted or withdrawn at any time — nothing you paid for depends on it, and local play never touches it. Do not use the relay to move traffic that is not a game session.
