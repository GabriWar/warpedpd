Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 July 2026

Warped Pixel Dungeon is a free, open-source roguelike developed by GabriWar. The short version: the game has no ads, no analytics, no accounts, and collects no personal data. Everything below explains the few network connections it does make and why.

Data stored on your device

Save files, settings, rankings and badges are written to your device's private app storage. They never leave the device, and removing the game deletes them. There is no cloud save and no account system.

Network connections the game makes

  • News and update checks. The game periodically fetches a public changelog feed and release information hosted on GitHub. These are ordinary web requests carrying no identifier, account or personal data. Both can be turned off in the game's settings.
  • Local-network multiplayer. When you host or join a co-op game, the game exchanges gameplay data — the nickname you type, hero state, positions and actions — with the other players' devices on your local network, for the duration of that session only. Nothing is sent to a server operated by the developer, and nothing is retained afterwards. On iOS the system asks for local network permission the first time you use this feature; declining it only disables multiplayer.
  • Subscription status check (iOS). To confirm whether a supporter subscription is still active, the app sends its App Store receipt to Apple's receipt-verification service (buy.itunes.apple.com). The receipt is issued by Apple and contains purchase information for this app only — no name, email or payment details. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

As with any internet request, the servers contacted above (GitHub and Apple) can see the IP address the request came from. That is inherent to how the internet works; the game itself neither logs nor transmits it anywhere else.

Purchases and donations

  • App Store (iOS). Optional supporter subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through In-App Purchase. The game never sees or receives your payment details — only Apple's confirmation that a purchase exists and whether it is still active.
  • Google Play (Android). Optional supporter donations are processed entirely by Google Play Billing, under the same terms. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Builds from GitHub and desktop. These have no store billing and instead link to Ko-fi, an external site with its own privacy policy. The game is not involved in those transactions. Store builds never show this link.

What the game does not do

  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics, telemetry or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No accounts, and no collection of names, email addresses, contacts, location or advertising identifiers.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no data sold or shared with third parties.

Children

The game collects no personal data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If the game's data handling changes, this page is updated and the date at the top revised. Material changes will also be noted in the in-game news feed.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact@gabriwar.xyz, or open an issue at the project's issue tracker.
